Mine operations analytics: trusted plant data, a quality soft sensor and gold mine safety

Two operational questions from public data. In a processing plant: can the data be trusted, and can concentrate quality be estimated between laboratory results? Across gold mines: where do injuries come from, and do inspection records point to the mines most at risk next year? Every model is tested on months or years it never saw, against the simple method an operation already uses.

    Which silica values are real measurements?

    Days shown

    Each point is one hour. Measured: a new value, constant through the hour. Repeat: the same value as the hour before. Varies within hour: the value changes from one 20-second row to the next, so it was interpolated. Straight-line fill: the hour lies exactly on a line between its neighbours; with values recorded to two decimals some of these may be real results that happened to change by equal steps, but they are left out to be safe.
